How does locking work?

Each of you has a small secret key, made once on the device you use. It never leaves that device. Messages and files are locked only when you both have a key.

All files are kept on our secure server. A locked file is unreadable there: only you and your friend can open it, not even we can. A file that is not locked can be read by our system.
