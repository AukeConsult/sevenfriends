Sound and notifications

Seven Friends is about being there for your closest friends. They can only reach you if you notice them, so set up how you want to be told when a friend writes or asks to connect.

Sounds

Choose the sound for each thing that can arrive. Sounds only play while Seven Friends is open, and your choices are saved on your account, so they follow you to every device.

Also turn up the volume of your device. Press Play on a sound to hear the new volume.

Notifications

When Seven Friends is open but you are not looking at it (another tab is selected, the window is minimized, or another window is in front), a message or friend request shows a notification on your screen. While you look at the app nothing pops up; only the sound plays. Click it to come back. The tab title also shows how many things are waiting. A closed app cannot notify you.

How do I allow notifications?

Your browser has been told to block notifications from this site. The switches here cannot change that: you allow it once in the browser, then come back and turn the switch on again.

Chrome or Edge on a computer
  1. Click the icon at the left end of the address bar (a lock or sliders).
  2. Find Notifications and set it to Allow.
  3. Reload this page, then turn the switch on again.
Or open chrome://settings/content/notifications (Edge: edge://settings/content/notifications) and move this site from Block to Allow.
Android
  1. Tap the icon next to the address, then Permissions → Notifications → Allow.
  2. Also check Android Settings → Apps → your browser → Notifications: it must be on.
iPhone
  1. Notifications only work from the Home Screen app: in Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen, and open the app from its icon.
  2. Check iPhone Settings → Notifications → Seven Friends → Allow Notifications.
  3. If you once tapped "Don't Allow", delete the Home Screen app, add it again and allow when asked.
Windows
  1. Settings → System → Notifications: turn notifications on, and turn on your browser in the list.
  2. Turn off Do not disturb or Focus assist.
