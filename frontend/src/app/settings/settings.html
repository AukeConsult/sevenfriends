Notifications

When Seven Friends is open but you are not looking at it (another tab is selected, the window is minimized, or another window is in front), a message or friend request shows a notification on your screen. While you look at the app nothing pops up; only the sound plays. Click it to come back. The tab title also shows how many things are waiting. A closed app cannot notify you.

Push notifications

The phone or computer wakes up for a new message or friend request, also when the app is closed or the screen is locked, and plays its own notification sound.
