Your data and privacy

Seven Friends is for your closest friends, so we keep as little as possible. This page explains in plain words what is stored, who can see it, which rights you have under the GDPR (the European data protection law), and how to delete things.

What is stored about you

  • Your login: how you sign in (Google, email or phone) and the email address or phone number you signed in with.
  • Your profile: your name, and what you choose to add: email, phone number, country, mood and mood message, social accounts, a picture or an icon.
  • Your friends: the friend list (seven seats and a sleep list), friend requests and the message that came with them.
  • Your conversations: messages, pictures, videos and voice messages you send and receive, and the preview of links you send.
  • Settings and status: your sound choices, whether you are active, passive or offline, the devices that receive your notifications, and your invite link code.

Everything is kept until you or the other person delete it, or until you delete your account. Nothing is sold, and there are no advertisements.

Who can see it

  • Your friends see your name, picture, mood and status. A friend who has not confirmed yet only sees what is needed to answer the request.
  • Your friends see the contact details you put on your profile (phone, email, social accounts). Leave a field empty if you do not want to share it.
  • If you have not verified your email or phone, your friends are told that, because someone else could be using it.
  • Only you and your friend can read your conversation.
  • Someone who knows your email address or phone number can send you a friend request.

The app runs on Google Firebase (login, database, file storage, hosting and notifications): your data is stored with Google on our behalf.

Your rights under the GDPR

  • To know and to see your data: your profile, friends and conversations are all visible in the app.
  • To correct it: change your name, contact details, country, mood and picture on your profile; edit a message you sent.
  • To have it erased: see "How to delete" below. You can erase a message, a friend, or everything.
  • To take it with you: there is no export button yet. Pictures, videos and voice messages can be downloaded one by one from the conversation.
  • To object or to restrict: you can stop any time: put a friend to sleep, turn off sounds and notifications, or delete your account.
  • To complain: you can complain to the data protection authority of your country (in Norway: Datatilsynet).

How to delete

  • One message: delete it in the conversation. It disappears for both of you.
  • Many messages: in the conversation, tap Delete at the top and choose by age, by who wrote them, or by a word. They disappear for both of you.
  • Your picture: remove or replace it on your profile.
  • A friend: delete the friend on the friends page. The friendship and the whole shared conversation, pictures and videos included, are erased on both sides.
  • Put a friend to sleep: the friend loses the seat but the conversation is kept. Delete the friend if you want it gone.
  • This device: logging out stops notifications on this device.
  • Everything: delete your account.

What deleting your account erases

Your login, your profile and picture, your friend list, every friend request you sent or received, and every message you sent or received, including the pictures, videos and voice messages, in your friends' conversations too. It is erased for good and cannot be undone. You are asked to confirm first.

What your friends already saw cannot be taken back: a friend may have downloaded a picture or copied a message to somewhere else, outside the app.
