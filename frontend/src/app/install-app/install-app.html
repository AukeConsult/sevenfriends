} @if (!app.installed() && steps()) {

This device needs a few steps. Follow the ones for your device:

iPhone or iPad (Safari)

An iPhone has no "Install" button, so you put the app on your home screen yourself. It takes less than a minute.

  1. Open Seven Friends in Safari, the blue compass app. This does not work from inside another app's browser (Facebook, Instagram, mail), so if you got a link there, copy it and paste it in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button: a square with an arrow pointing up. It is at the bottom of the screen on an iPhone, and at the top right on an iPad.
  3. Scroll down in the list that opens and tap Add to Home Screen. If you do not see it, scroll further, or tap More or View More first.
  4. You can keep the name Seven Friends. If you are asked, leave Open as Web App switched on. Tap Add in the top right corner.
  5. Go to your home screen: the Seven Friends icon is there, like any other app. Open it from that icon, not from Safari. Only the icon opens it as an app.
  6. Sign in again the first time. The app on the home screen keeps its own login, separate from Safari.
  7. When it asks to send you notifications, tap Allow, or your friends cannot reach you when the app is closed.

Notifications on an iPhone need iOS 16.4 or newer (Settings → General → Software Update shows your version). To change them later: Settings → Notifications → Seven Friends. To remove the app: press and hold the icon and choose Remove App.

Computer (Chrome, Edge or Safari)
  1. Open Seven Friends in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar (a screen with a small arrow), or open the browser menu and choose Install Seven Friends (Chrome: under Cast, save and share).
  3. Click Install. The app opens in its own window and gets an icon in your app list.

Safari on a Mac: choose File → Add to Dock.
