Install the app

What is installing?

Seven Friends is a web page that can also live on your device like any other app. "Installing" does not download anything big and does not use the app store: your browser puts the same app in your app menu or on your home screen.

  • It has its own icon, and opens full screen without the browser's address bar.
  • You can find it again in one tap, so you notice your friends sooner.
  • It is the same app and the same account: your friends and messages are all there, and the app updates itself.
  • You can remove it at any time like any other app. That does not delete your account.

Installing is optional: the app works in the browser too.
