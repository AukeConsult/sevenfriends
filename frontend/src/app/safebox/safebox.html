What is this?

Your files are always kept private: only you and {{ friendName() }} can open them. Locking (encryption) goes one step further. The file is scrambled on your own device before it is sent, so nobody else can read it, not even the people who run the servers. Only you two can unscramble it.

Who can lock files?

Locking needs a small secret key on each side. A key is made once, on the device you use, and it never leaves it.
