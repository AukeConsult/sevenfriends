About Seven Friends

A place for your closest friends

Seven Friends is a calm, simple app for staying in touch with the seven friends who matter most to you. Seven seats, no feed, no noise: you notice your friends, and they notice you.

Made for free use

Seven Friends is made for free use. There is nothing to pay, there are no advertisements, and your data is not sold.

Who made it

Seven Friends is made by Blueboot R&D AS, Norway.

Questions, ideas, problems or requests about your data: write to hey@blueboot.ai.
